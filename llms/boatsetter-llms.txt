# Boatsetter

> Boatsetter is a peer-to-peer boat rental marketplace connecting boat owners with renters and USCG-licensed captains. Founded 2012, headquartered in Miami (originally Fort Lauderdale), with 50,000+ boats across 600-700 locations worldwide. Boatsetter publishes no public developer API, OpenAPI definition, SDK, or developer portal — the surfaces below are human-facing web and mobile applications.
